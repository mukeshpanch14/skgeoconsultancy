What we do

Complete services for mines, quarries & landowners

From the first survey of your land to statutory returns year after year, every step is handled under one roof.

01

Topographical Services

GPS, DGPS and Electronic Total Station (ETS) surveys for boundaries, contours and pit levels.

02

Mining & Quarry Plans / Scheme of Mining

Mining plans, quarry plans and schemes of mining prepared by an RQP, ready for submission.

03

Environmental Clearance & Pollution Works

Consent for Establishment (CFE) and Consent for Operation (CFO) applications with supporting reports.

04

Pre-Feasibility Reports

Early assessment of a deposit's viability to support lease and investment decisions.

05

Reserves, Resources & Geological Mapping

Estimation of mineral reserves and resources with detailed geological mapping of the lease area.

06

Annual & Monthly Returns

Timely preparation and filing of statutory returns so your operations stay compliant.

07

Core Drilling & Chemical Analysis

Core drilling works and chemical analysis of samples to establish grade and quality.

08

Ground Water Bore Points & Investigation

Hydrogeological investigation to locate reliable bore points before you drill.

09

Ground Water Testing & Analysis

Water quality testing and analysis for drinking, agricultural and industrial use.

Survey & mapping

Accurate field data is the foundation of every approval

Every mining plan we prepare starts on the ground. Boundaries, contours and pit levels are captured with GPS, DGPS and ETS, then turned into the maps and estimates the department expects.

  • Lease boundary & topographical surveys
  • Geological mapping & reserve estimation
  • Mining plans & schemes of mining by an RQP
Drilling & groundwater

Know what lies beneath before you invest

Core drilling and chemical analysis establish the grade of your deposit. For farms, industries and layouts, our groundwater investigations identify reliable bore points and test water quality before you spend on drilling.

  • Core drilling with sample chemical analysis
  • Ground water bore point identification
  • Water testing & analysis reports
